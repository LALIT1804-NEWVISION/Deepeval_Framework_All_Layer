3. Order Placement Requirements

To place an order, customers must provide a valid delivery address and select a supported payment method.

The store supports the following payment methods:

Credit cards
Debit cards
UPI
Net banking

Customers can use a previously saved delivery address or provide a new valid delivery address.

4. Shipping Policies

The store provides free shipping on eligible orders.

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days.
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.

Shipping eligibility and delivery times may depend on the order and delivery location.

5. Order Tracking

Customers can track their orders using the order tracking page and tracking number.

Customers can check their order status using the order tracking page.

6. Order Cancellation Policy

Customers can cancel their orders from the order details page before the orders are shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation is not available through the standard order cancellation process.

For assistance with cancellation-related questions, customers can contact customer support.

7. Return Policy

Customers can return products within 30 days if the products are unused and in their original condition.

Products that do not meet these eligibility requirements may not qualify for a return.

Customers should contact customer support for assistance with return eligibility and the return process.

8. Refund Policy

Eligible returned products are refunded according to the store's refund policy.

Refund eligibility depends on the product meeting the return policy requirements.

Customers should contact customer support for information about refund status and processing.

The exact refund processing time and refund method are not specified in this knowledge base.

9. Customer Support

The store provides customer support for the following topics:

Payment-related questions
Shipping and delivery
Order tracking and order status
Order cancellation
Product returns
Refund-related questions

Customers can contact customer support for assistance with these topics.

10. Handling Unknown Information

If the requested information is not explicitly available in this knowledge base, the assistant must not invent details or make unsupported assumptions.

The assistant should clearly state that the requested information is not available in the knowledge base and recommend contacting customer support when appropriate.

The assistant must not invent:

Payment methods that are not listed
Shipping times that are not specified
Cancellation exceptions that are not documented
Return eligibility rules beyond those specified
Exact refund processing times or refund methods that are not provided

All answers must be grounded in the retrieved knowledge base context.
